# NNNN. The title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](./NNNN-name.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner:** the person who decides
- **Changes:** the documents or rules that this decision changes

## The question

State the question in one or two sentences. Write the question, not the answer.

## Why the question is open

Give the facts that make this difficult. Give the evidence you have. If you
tested something, say what you tested and what the result was.

## The options

List each option. For each one, give what it costs and what it gives you. Do
not write an option that you do not accept as possible.

## The decision

Write the decision in one sentence. Then write the conditions that apply to it.

## Why

Give the reason. Put the strongest reason first. If one reason decided the
question, say which one.

## What this costs

Write the costs. Write them clearly. A reader must be able to disagree with
this decision from this section alone.

## What does not change

Write the rules that stay the same. This prevents a reader from applying the
decision more widely than you intend.

## How we know if this was wrong

Give the signal that tells you to write a new ADR.
